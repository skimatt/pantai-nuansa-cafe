## Storyboard completion criteria
Setiap shot mempunyai: storyboard frame, scene ID, asset IDs, depth ordering, camera start/end, scroll range/duration, audio cues, mobile composition, fallback reduced motion, test cases. Durasi opening adalah target awal, bukan angka penelitian.
