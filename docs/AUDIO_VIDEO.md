# Audio & Video Direction

## Audio
Ocean ambience, soft wind, restrained click/transition cues; optional narration. Mute default; browser audio unlock only on user gesture. Provide toggle, preserve preference for session, pause/cleanup when hidden. Do not rely on audio for essential information.

## Video
Real Pantai Nuansa Cafe footage when provided; poster image, explicit controls, captions/subtitles when dialogue exists, preload metadata/none, lazy loading. Muted short ambient video may autoplay inline if policy allows; meaningful video with audio requires user action. Avoid universal frame-by-frame scroll scrubbing due decoding cost; only use pre-optimized clips if tested.

## Transition
Ambient crossfade per zone, no loud effects, no abrupt volume jumps, no automatic narration. Audio design should reinforce narrative and remain optional.

## Media provenance
Track creator, date, usage rights, consent for identifiable people where necessary, source. Never fabricate real venue footage with AI and present as documentary.
